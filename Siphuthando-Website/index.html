WHO WE ARE

Care rooted in love, respect and community.

Siphuthando Home Based Care and Day Care Service Centre is a non-profit organisation established on 1 June 1999 by Mrs Joyce Mazibuko.

We are committed to improving the lives of older people through care, support, day-care services and community development.

Our work is guided by honesty, respect, integrity and the belief that every person deserves to be treated with dignity.

EVERYONE DESERVES CARE

A community where everyone belongs.

We believe that quality care should be accessible to everyone, regardless of background, gender, race or disability.

Our organisation supports older people who are vulnerable, frail or living with disabilities. We strive to create an environment where everyone is welcomed, respected and valued.
